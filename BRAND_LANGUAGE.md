## 2. Voice

**Tone (Deliverable 01):** confident, local, practical, direct, and human.

**Personality (brand guide):** knowledgeable, simple, direct, honest,
reassuring, professional, modern with institutional credibility.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Speak plainly and specifically: "what the market, regulators, banks, and partners will actually require" | Sound like a generic consulting firm or an "automated AI report factory" |
| Be honest about limits: "Bank approval is never guaranteed." | Promise outcomes Integra cannot control (approvals, timelines, funding) |
| Lead with local judgment and practical execution | Rely on buzzwords ("world-class", "cutting-edge", "revolutionary") |
| Address the reader as "you" | Talk about Integra in the third person in calls to action |
| Keep sentences short and concrete | Use long, abstract paragraphs |

## 3. Audience

Use these names consistently:

- **U.S.-based founders**: Black and African-American founders, operators, and investors in the U.S. exploring Qatar and the GCC (design brief).
- **African founders**: founders and growth-stage companies from Nigeria, Morocco, and across the continent (Deliverable 01).
- **Diaspora and international investors** (Deliverable 01).
- **Referral partners**: law firms, banks, trade missions, embassies, accelerators, free-zone contacts, and ecosystem partners (Deliverable 01).

## 4. Legal guardrails

Integra provides business, market-entry, regulatory-navigation, and
relationship-development advisory. It is **not a law firm** and does not give
legal advice.

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| regulatory readiness, regulatory navigation, compliance readiness | legal support, legal advice, legal services |
| licensed professional referrals | "our lawyers", "we will represent you" |
| coordination, readiness, pathway | guarantee, guaranteed approval |

The disclaimer must stay in the footer and on the intake form.

## 7. Style

- **Spelling:** U.S. English (organize, program, center), matching the documents.
- **Names:** Integra Advisory Partners (full), Integra (short). Never "Aun Advisory".
- **Places and terms:** Qatar, Doha, the GCC, Saudi Arabia, UAE, KYC, visa/Iqama.
- **Headings:** sentence case ("Expanding into the GCC is not just a market-research exercise.").
- **Prices:** "$5,000+", "$2,500 / month+", always labelled "Indicative price".
